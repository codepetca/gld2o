# PPZ3C — Health for Life (Asynchronous Online)

This package contains:
- Course outline with pacing and release cadence
- Pika-ready assignments (A1–A10) organized into 5 units
- Universal rubric (/30) used for all submissions
- Resources folder (templates, guides)

Health for Life (PPZ3C) helps you examine the factors that influence your own
health practices and behaviours, and those that contribute to healthy
communities. The course emphasizes **wellness** — physical, cognitive,
emotional, spiritual, and social — and centres on a **Personal Wellness
Project** you develop and update across the whole course.

## Pacing & Weekly Rhythm
- Course runs ~18 instructional weeks (adjust for breaks and holidays)
- Daily: students complete a short Pika check-in while progressing on the current assignment.
- Weekly: post a short video (2–4 minutes) previewing the plan, deadlines, and any clarifications.

## Units & Assignment Sequence

### Unit 1: Getting Started
- Week 1: A1 Getting Started (5%)
- Week 2: A2 Personal Wellness Project Proposal (10%)

### Unit 2: Wellness & Mental Health
- Week 3: A3 Dimensions of Wellness & Health Literacy (5%)
- Week 4: A4 Mental Health & Stress Management (5%)

### Unit 3: Healthy Living — Nutrition & Fitness
- Weeks 5–6: A5 Nutrition & Healthy Eating (10%)
- Weeks 7–8: A6 Physical Activity & Fitness (10%)

### Unit 4: Health & Community
- Weeks 9–10: A7 Personal Wellness Project Progress Update (5%)
- Weeks 11–12: A8 Substance Use, Safety & Healthy Relationships (5%)
- Weeks 13–14: A9 Healthy Communities — Health-Promotion Campaign (15%)

### Unit 5: Showcase
- Weeks 15–18: A10 Final Portfolio & Personal Wellness Plan — Google Site (30%)

Final portfolio due end of Week 18.

- A1 Getting Started: 5%

- A4 Mental Health & Stress Management: 5%
- A5 Nutrition & Healthy Eating: 10%
- A6 Physical Activity & Fitness: 10%
- A7 Personal Wellness Project Progress Update: 5%
- A8 Substance Use, Safety & Healthy Relationships: 5%
- A9 Healthy Communities — Health-Promotion Campaign: 15%
- A10 Final Portfolio & Personal Wellness Plan (Google Site): 30%
- **Total: 100%**
- All use the universal /30 rubric (Completion, Thinking/Communication, Workflow/Authenticity).

## Submission Expectations
- All assignments (except A10) are completed in Pika using the assignments feature.
- A10 Final Portfolio is a Google Site; students submit the URL in Pika.
- At the end of the course, final portfolio links will be shared with classmates.
- Pika tracks history automatically; encourage students to show their process.
