## A2 Personal Wellness Project Proposal [DRAFT]
Due: 2026-02-13

Purpose: Propose a personal wellness project you'll track through the course (e.g., improve your sleep, build a fitness routine, cook healthier meals, manage stress, cut down screen time, train for a 5K). This project becomes the spine of your Personal Wellness Plan and your final portfolio.

What to do:
1) What matters to your health right now: List areas of your wellness you'd like to improve (physical, emotional/mental, social, sleep, nutrition, activity, screen time).

2) Lead-up questions (answer briefly):
   - What's one habit you wish you were better at?
   - When do you feel most energized and "at your best"?
   - What gets in the way of being healthy for you right now?
   - How much time per week could you realistically give this?

3) Your proposal and plan: Describe the wellness project you want to pursue and set personal deadlines for yourself. Include:
   - Why it matters to you
   - What "healthier" looks like by course end (a specific, measurable goal)
   - A rough timeline with milestones

   It's okay if you don't know all the steps yet. If your goal is new to you, your first milestone might just be researching and tracking your current habits. Your plan will probably change as you learn more — that's expected. The point is to start with a direction and adjust as you go.

   Example:
   I want to improve my sleep. I usually stay up late on my phone and feel tired all week. I want to change that.
   - February: track my current sleep and screen time for two weeks
   - March: set a consistent bedtime and a no-phone-in-bed rule
   - April: add a wind-down routine and measure how I feel
   - May: aim for 8 hours on school nights and reflect on the change

4) A typical session: Describe what working on this project actually looks like day to day. What would you do? How often?

5) Resources: List at least 2 credible resources (people, sites, apps, videos, AI) you could use.

How to submit:
- Complete this assignment in Pika.

Approximate length: 1500 characters (300 words)

Success criteria (/30):
- Completion: lead-up questions, proposal with a specific measurable goal and timeline, session description, and resources are present.
- Thinking/communication: proposal is specific, realistic, and personal; goals are reasonably specific.
- Workflow/authenticity: your own words.

## A3 Dimensions of Wellness & Health Literacy [DRAFT]
Due: 2026-02-23

Purpose: Understand wellness as more than "not being sick," and practice judging whether health information is trustworthy.

What to do:
1) Dimensions of wellness: Wellness has several dimensions — physical, emotional/mental, social, spiritual, and cognitive. For 3 of these dimensions:
   - Describe what it means in your own words
   - Rate yourself out of 5 and explain why
   - Name one thing that would move you up half a point

2) Health literacy — checking a source:
   Health information is everywhere, and a lot of it is wrong or trying to sell you something. Find one health claim online (a post, ad, video, or article — e.g., a supplement, diet, or fitness "hack"). Record the link, then judge it:
   - Who made it, and do they have real qualifications?
   - Is it selling something? Is it current? Does it cite evidence?
   - Would you trust it? Why or why not?
   - Find one credible source (e.g., a government or health-organization page) that confirms or contradicts the claim, and link it.

3) Reflection (4–6 sentences): Which wellness dimension is your strongest? Which needs the most work? How does being able to spot bad health information protect you?

How to submit:
- Complete this assignment in Pika.

Approximate length: 1200 characters (250 words)

Success criteria (/30):
- Completion: 3 wellness dimensions rated, a health claim evaluated with a credible source, and a reflection are present.
- Thinking/communication: evaluation of the source is specific; reasoning is clear.
- Workflow/authenticity: organized with headings, links to sources, your own words.

## A9 Healthy Communities — Health-Promotion Campaign [DRAFT]
Due: 2026-05-15

Purpose: Learn what makes a community healthy, then design a short campaign that could influence and support others in making a positive health choice — using AI to help you draft and refine it.

What to do:
1) In your Pika assignment, set up one long document with these headings, in this order:
   - Health Issue & Audience
   - Research
   - Campaign Message & Materials
   - Distribution Plan
   - Reflection
   - Revision Notes

2) Pick your issue and audience:
   - Choose one health issue that matters in a community you belong to (school, team, family, neighbourhood, online) — e.g., sleep, vaping, screen time, hydration, mental-health stigma, active transport.
   - Name the specific audience you want to reach (e.g., Grade 9s, your team, gamers).

3) Research (use at least 2 credible sources, keep the links): Record why this issue matters, who it affects, and what actually helps. Note the exact page URLs under "Research."

4) Campaign message & materials (use AI to iterate):

   - Then keep revising in stages: replace the previous version completely with your improved version, and make sure each revision is saved in the history.

   - Your Pika history should clearly show several revisions, each getting closer to something you'd actually put up.
   - Briefly note what you changed between versions and why under "Revision Notes."

5) Distribution plan: Where and how would this campaign reach your audience? List 2–3 realistic channels and why they fit.

6) Reflection (4–6 sentences):

7) Suggested resources:
   - Health Canada: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada.html
