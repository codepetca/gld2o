# PPZ3C — Health for Life (Grade 11, College Preparation)

Ministry course code: **PPZ3C** • Credit value: 1.0 • Prerequisite: none
Source: Ontario Ministry of Education, *The Ontario Curriculum, Grades 9–12:
Health and Physical Education* (2015). This file is a working summary of the
curriculum used to plan this course; consult the official curriculum document
for the full text of every expectation.

## Course Description
Health for Life enables students to examine the factors that influence their
own health practices and behaviours, as well as those factors that contribute
to the development of healthy communities. It emphasizes the concept of
**wellness** — which addresses all aspects of well-being (physical, cognitive,
emotional, spiritual, and social) — and promotes healthy eating, physical
activity, and the building and maintaining of a positive sense of self.
Students use what they learn to develop and implement a **personal wellness
plan** and to encourage others to lead healthy, active lives.

## Overall Expectations (working summary)
By the end of this course, students will:

1. **Health information & decision-making** — demonstrate the ability to assess
   the quality of health information and use credible health information to make
   informed decisions and take appropriate action about matters affecting their
   health.
2. **Personal wellness** — demonstrate an understanding of the concept of
   personal wellness, the factors that influence it, and ways of maintaining and
   enhancing it.
3. **Personal wellness plan** — demonstrate the ability to develop and implement
   a personal wellness plan.
4. **Healthy communities** — demonstrate an understanding of the components of
   healthy communities and the factors that affect and sustain health within
   them.
5. **Influencing others** — demonstrate the ability to influence and support
   others in making positive health choices.

Throughout, students apply **living skills** — personal skills,
interpersonal skills, and critical and creative thinking — as they learn.

## Topic Areas Covered
- Concept of wellness and its dimensions; health literacy (assessing sources)
- Mental health, stress management, and reducing stigma
- Healthy eating and nutrition (Canada's Food Guide, food myths)
- Physical activity, fitness components, and movement guidelines
- Substance use, addictions, and informed decision-making
- Personal safety and injury prevention
- Healthy relationships and interpersonal skills
- Healthy communities and health promotion
