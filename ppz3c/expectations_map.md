# PPZ3C Expectations Map (internal)

Maps each assignment to the PPZ3C overall expectations (see
`PPZ3C_curriculum.md`). Overall expectations:

1. Health information & decision-making
2. Personal wellness (concept & factors)
3. Personal wellness plan (develop & implement)
4. Healthy communities
5. Influencing & supporting others

## Notes
- **The Personal Wellness Plan throughline (A2 → A7 → A10)** carries overall
  expectation 3 (develop and implement a personal wellness plan) — the
  signature culminating requirement of PPZ3C.
- **Health literacy** (overall expectation 1) is introduced explicitly in A3
  and reinforced whenever students cite and evaluate sources (A5, A6, A8, A9).
- **Healthy communities + influencing others** (overall expectations 4 & 5) are
  concentrated in A9's health-promotion campaign and revisited in A10.
- All assignments use the universal /30 rubric (Completion, Thinking/
  Communication, Workflow/Authenticity).
