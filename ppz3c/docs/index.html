PPZ3C — Health for Life

Course Overview

This course helps you examine the factors that influence your own health practices, as well as those that build healthy communities. You'll explore wellness, mental health, nutrition, physical activity, and personal safety, and build a Personal Wellness Plan you develop across the whole course.

Teacher: Mr. Chan

Format: Asynchronous Online • Grade 11 • College Preparation • Ontario Curriculum

Duration: ~18 instructional weeks

Submissions: All assignments except the last culminating project are completed in Pika.

Portfolio sharing: At the end of the course, your portfolio will be shared with the class.

Assignments

Unit 1: Getting Started

A1
Getting Started
Practice screenshots and try an AI tool.
5% • Wk 1
A2
Personal Wellness Project Proposal
Propose a personal wellness goal you'll track through the course.
10% • Wk 2

Unit 2: Wellness & Mental Health

A3
Wellness Wheel & Health Literacy
Self-assess your wellness dimensions and judge whether online health info is trustworthy.
5% • Wk 3
A4
Mental Health & Digital Wellbeing
Reflect on stress and coping, and audit how your screen-time habits affect your mood.
5% • Wk 4

Unit 3: Healthy Living — Nutrition & Fitness

A5
Nutrition: Food Log & Media Deconstruction
Track a day of eating against Canada's Food Guide and deconstruct food/diet media.
10% • Wk 5–6
A6
Physical Activity & Home Fitness Plan
Learn the components of fitness, then build, try, and log a 1-week home activity plan.
10% • Wk 7–8

Unit 4: Health & Community

A7
Personal Wellness Project Progress Update
Spend two weeks working on your A2 project, then submit a short, honest update on progress and next steps.
5% • Wk 9–10
A8
Substance Use & Personal Safety
Make informed decisions about risks and deconstruct how they're marketed to youth.
5% • Wk 11–12
A9
Digital Health-Promotion Campaign
Design a digital campaign that supports others in making a positive health choice, using AI.
15% • Wk 13–14

Unit 5: Showcase

A10
Final Portfolio & Personal Wellness Plan
Build a Google Site showcasing all your work and your wellness plan, starting with a setup week for planning and site building.
30% • Wk 15–18
